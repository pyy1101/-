About Unix Timestamp Converter

Unix timestamps are the standard way computers represent time, counting seconds from January 1, 1970. Developers frequently need to convert timestamps to readable dates when debugging APIs, checking database records, or analyzing logs. This tool supports bidirectional conversion for both second and millisecond timestamps. The current timestamp is displayed in real-time for quick reference.
